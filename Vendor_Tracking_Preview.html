Country/City Tracking Fix

Kaha: config.php → mlBWO() function (behind the scenes, koi UI change nahi)
Problem tha: Country column bahut zyada "—" dikha raha tha kyunki geo-lookup sirf ek provider (ip-api.com) use kar raha tha, jiski free limit 45 req/min hai — jyada traffic pe silently fail ho raha tha.

Fix: Ab 3 providers ka fallback chain hai — ip-api.com fail/rate-limit ho to turant ipwho.is try hota hai, phir ipinfo.io. Sirf tab khaali rehta hai jab teeno fail ho jaayein. 200+ country codes ki mapping bhi add hui taaki format hamesha consistent rahe ("India" — abbreviation nahi).

Prescreener — Country + Local Currency Income

Kaha: prescreener.php → naya Question 4
Kya karta hai: Respondent se poocha jaata hai "Which country are you in?" (IP se auto-detect hoke pehle se select bhi hota hai, respondent khud badal sakta hai). Uske turant baad wala Income question apne aap us country ki currency mein convert ho jaata hai — symbol + rounded amount ke saath. Zaroori: Database mein hamesha USD wala standard bracket hi save hota hai (e.g. "Under $25K") — sirf respondent ko dikhne wala label badalta hai, taaki reports/exports mein cross-country comparison kabhi na tootey.
